Methodology

What is Solkoff?

Solkoff is the average opponent points per game (PPG) — it measures the difficulty of a team's schedule, not its results. A higher value means a tougher run of opponents.

Strength = (Points% × Solkoff) / 100 Results × schedule difficulty
